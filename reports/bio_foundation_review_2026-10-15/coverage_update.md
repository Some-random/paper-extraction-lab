The earlier table was incomplete. This follow-up searched biological/scientific continued pretraining and followed references from NatureLM/BioMatrix into later models and corpora. TheBioCollection's Figure 2 also splits its 52.6B corpus into **33.8B free-text / 18.9B instruction stream**; rounded components sum to 52.7B. “Free-text” here includes rendered database records and tool-derived biological descriptions, not just journal articles or scraped webpages. Literature in Table 4 is counted separately. The inspected figure was read directly, not estimated from its arc sizes. [Primary report](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08803v1)

BioMamba's downstream clinical-note experiments are outside our data scope. The literature-CPT mixture is included because it directly addresses abstracts versus general replay. The 2026 published Mamba2 recipe should not be silently conflated with numbers from the earlier 2024 BioMamba preprint. The table retains the source's internal count/proportion discrepancy rather than repairing it. [Published report](https://spj.science.org/doi/10.34133/hds.0454)

I also checked [UniGenX](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.06687v1) and its [current official repository](https://github.com/UniGenX-Team/UniGenX). It is relevant to scientific sequence/coordinate generation; its structural sample counts should not become paper-extraction token budgets. Protein/docking support in later repository versions must be kept distinct from the inspected v1's molecule/material experiments.

**Effect on our acquisition plan**

The added reports strengthen the case for retaining original literature and biological source records. They do not require generated figure captions. The user has now explicitly selected **original figures, no model training** and **500 papers total**. The simple acquisition branch therefore keeps PDF/XML, original images, full source captions, referring paragraphs and article tables. It does not add synthetic reasoning, recaptioning, a VLM training stage, a web crawl or bulk database ingestion.
