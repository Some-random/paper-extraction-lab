# BiomedCLIP / PMC-15M

[Shared literature catalog](../README.md)

Biomedical image-text model

This page consolidates existing reviewed sources. Author-reported results have not been reproduced here.

**Training stage:** Contrastive image-text pretraining

**Source material and units:** **15,282,336 image–caption pairs**, collected from a 4.4M-article PMC snapshot

**Acquisition and document processing:** Pairs original PMC figures with author captions for contrastive training; not a requirement to recaption them with a teacher VLM
