Each example shows the question, the information needed to answer it, the choices and the worked answer. Sources, evidence checks and limitations expand below each answer.

<summary>Selection criteria and evidence checks</summary>

The examples were selected for a supported answer, fair input, a reusable biological lesson, clear substantive reasoning and variety. Eight completed answers were reused and two were newly generated. The new questions examine binding valency and the interpretation of localization controls.

All ten selected answers agree with their recorded source evidence. Explanatory steps can include qualified biological inferences and mathematical deductions; the cited papers do not prove every sentence. Reviews are assistant checks, not expert human ground truth. Training gains have not been measured.

Some questions use sequence and choices alone; others supply the published observations needed for experimental reasoning. Full papers and private reference labels were withheld during solving and used for later checking. Source qualifications are retained with each example. These examples are not a reproduction of the LiveProteinBench evaluation set.

The two new generations cost **$0.70678**. Reusing the eight previous answers required no additional generation.
